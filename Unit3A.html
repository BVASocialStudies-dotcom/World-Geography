World Geography unit vocabulary

Migration

Migration is the movement of people from one place to another. Learn these terms, from the reasons people leave home to the ways they settle somewhere new.

Economic Migration

Moving to a new place to find better jobs, higher pay, or a better standard of living.

Seasonal/Cyclical Migration

Moving back and forth between places on a regular schedule, often to follow work like crop harvests or to escape harsh weather.

Chain Migration

When people move to a place because family members or friends from their home community already live there and can help them settle in.

Refugees

People who are forced to flee their home country because of war, violence, or persecution and cannot safely return.

Reluctant Migration

Moving when a person does not really want to leave but feels they have little choice, such as after losing a job or a natural disaster.

Diaspora

A group of people who have spread out from their original homeland to many other places but still share a common culture and identity.

Internal Migration

Moving from one place to another within the same country, such as from a rural area to a city.
